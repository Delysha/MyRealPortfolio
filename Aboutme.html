About Me

Hello, I’m Délysha Jannella Orsel, a Software Developer from Amsterdam, currently studying at Media College Amsterdam. At the moment, my primary focus is working with C# in Unity Engine, and blueprints in Unreal Engine but my long-term goal is to dive into VR game development.

I’m especially passionate about specializing in VR/Gameplay Development because I aim to create VR games that can help children facing challenges. My ultimate goal is to focus on mental health and well-being through the immersive experiences VR can provide.

I’m drawn to VR because I truly believe it represents the future of the gaming industry. With the continuous growth of technology, I imagine that in about 10 years, VR games will be much more prevalent than traditional games on devices like laptops. It’s an exciting area that not only aligns with my career goals but is also something I find incredibly fun and fascinating to learn about.

While VR is my main interest, I’m also skilled in web development, working with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I consider myself more of an all-rounder in development, rather than just focusing on one aspect like game or web development. My aim is to keep improving across various coding languages while maintaining a primary focus on VR. It’s important to me to stay versatile because, as I grow, my interests may evolve, and having a broad skill set opens up more possibilities in the future.

In my free time, I enjoy playing video games, reading, watching movies, and programming. I also love creating small games in Unreal Engine to sharpen my skills.

For now, my focus is clear: becoming a VR developer, but I’m open to learning and growing across different areas of development as well.
